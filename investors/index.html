Be part of the journey

Trusted care for the families who can't always be there.

Trusbee is on a mission to bring verified, on-demand care to every tier-2 family in India, starting in Patna. The product is built, verified Bees are on the ground, and the model is being proven city by city. If the vision resonates, we would like to talk.

Why this, why now

A trust problem that WhatsApp can finally solve.

Millions of parents, children far away

India's families are spread across Bengaluru, Delhi, Dubai and beyond, and Bihar's most of all. Money travels in seconds; a trusted pair of hands does not.

"Verified" has meant nothing

The informal attendant market runs on word of mouth. Trusbee's six-check ladder, uniform and photo ID make verification the product.

No app to install

Our own WhatsApp booking assistant, built in-house in English, Hindi and Bhojpuri, meets families where they already are. PIN-verified start and end make every hour billable and auditable.

Bees earn with dignity

Fixed base plus hourly pay, weekly payouts, insurance and a promotion ladder. Supply that stays is the moat.
