Questions about our rates

What are your home care rates?

Every care service has four rates: hourly, 8 hours, day care and 24 hours. Care starts from ₹199 per hour. Nursing procedures and Home ICU Setup have their own rates, all listed on this page.

Is there a travel charge?

Travel charge is ₹7.80 per km above 5 km.

How do I pay?

Our team confirms the final amount and the payment method on WhatsApp or phone before the care begins.
