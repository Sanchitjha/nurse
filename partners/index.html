How a partnership works

Simple for your desk. Safe for their family.

You refer, we respond

Your staff share one number with the family, or message us directly with the ward and time. A verified Bee is assigned within minutes.

Same six checks, same PIN control

Every Bee is Aadhaar-checked, police-verified, reference-checked, interviewed and trained. The family controls the start and end with PINs, and sees every step on WhatsApp.

One bill, clear records

Hourly billing with GST shown separately, paid by the family after the visit or consolidated to your institution monthly, with a booking-by-booking statement.

A founder on call

Any concern on your premises reaches our founders directly. We work alongside your staff and never step into medical care.
