A wedding, a family visit, a day of hospital appointments, or your regular driver on leave? Book a police-verified Drive Bee for the whole day at one fixed price. Finishing early does not change it; extra time is billed by the hour.

What a day with a Drive Bee includes

  • One police-verified driver for the whole block, from the time you choose
  • Waiting at hospitals, functions, offices and shops is included
  • Time beyond the plan is billed by the hour at ₹219 + GST
  • No driver works more than 12 hours in a row, for your safety on the road
  • Your car's fuel, parking and tolls stay with you, at actual cost

A driver every day, or for a month

  • Book daily or recurring drives in the WhatsApp booking form: every day, Monday to Saturday, weekdays or your own days, at the same time.
  • With 3 or more days a week, the service price is 10% lower.
  • A ₹40 travel charge applies per day, and you are billed weekly.
  • We try to keep the same driver for a regular booking.
Questions about this service
How much does a driver cost per day in Patna?

An Extended Drive of 9 hours is ₹899 and a Full Shift Drive of 12 hours is ₹1,149, each + 18% GST. Night plans cost a little more. Fuel, parking and tolls are yours.

What if the day runs longer than planned?

Extra time is billed by the hour at ₹219 + GST and shows on the bill. If you already know the day will be long, book the 12-hour plan.

Can I book a driver for a wedding day?

Yes. Many families book a Full Shift Drive for the wedding day, or a night plan for the reception. Tell the bot the venue and the timings.

Can I get a monthly driver?

Yes, as a recurring booking: choose your days and time in the WhatsApp booking form. With 3 or more days a week the service price is 10% lower, and you are billed weekly.
