Home care services in Your City

Sewa Saathi Home Care brings trained nurses, caregivers and attendants to your home in Your City. Whether a parent needs daily support, a patient is recovering after surgery, or a newborn needs gentle care, our team looks after your loved ones in the comfort of their own home.

Our services include patient care, elder care, critical care, Alzheimer's care, Parkinson's care, tracheostomy care, baby care and attendant services, along with nursing procedures such as Foley catheter, IV and IM injections, and complete Home ICU Setup. Every care service has clear rates: hourly, 8 hours, day care and 24 hours.

Help centre

Questions, answered

How do I book?

Message or call us, or fill the short form on this page. You see the rate before you confirm, and our team confirms everything on WhatsApp.

How do your rates work?

Every care service has four rates: hourly, 8 hours, day care and 24 hours. Choose what fits the need. The full rate list is on this page.

Is there a travel charge?

Travel charge is ₹7.80 per km above 5 km.

Can I ask for a female caregiver?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will confirm who is available.

Do you give injections or place catheters?

Yes. Foley catheter, IV and IM injections are done by trained staff at home, only on your doctor's prescription.

What is Home ICU Setup?

A complete ICU set up at home with 2 nursing staff and 1 general duty assistant (GDA). Message us and our team will explain what is included.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes, just message us on WhatsApp. We will help you extend, change or stop the care.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Your City and nearby areas. Message us your location and we will confirm.
