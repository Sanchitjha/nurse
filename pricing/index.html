Trusbee/Pricing
Care plans and hourly rates

Two ways to book. One promise on the price.

Pay by the hour for the time your Bee actually works, or take a fixed-price care plan and pay less. Every price below is before GST, and GST at 18% is always a separate line on your bill.

A plan is one fixed price for the whole block. Finishing early does not reduce it, and that is the trade for the lower price. Time beyond the plan is charged by the hour.

The price promise

You never pay more than the plan.

Book by the hour and you still get plan pricing. Whichever care plan matches the hours you booked becomes the most you pay for that block, so a long booking that finishes early is billed at the plan price, not the meter.

You are never charged above the plan that covers the hours you actually used. Under three hours, normal hourly rules apply and a 50% minimum charge is in place, because a Bee was reserved for you.

  • Book 5 hours, done in 2You pay Quick Care, not five hours on the meter.
  • Book 8 hours, done in 4You pay Half Day Care.
  • Book 8 hours, used 8You pay Extended Care, not eight hours on the meter.
  • Book 12 hours, used 12You pay Full Shift Care.
  • Booked under 3 hoursNormal hourly rules, with a 50% minimum charge.

Good to know

Fees and the fine print

  • Bee Arrival Fee ₹0–99 is confirmed when your Bee is assigned, based on distance. Return support is free when the visit ends where it started. If it ends somewhere else, a ₹49 return fee applies. GST does not apply to the arrival or return fee.
  • GST 18% extra on every price on this page, shown as CGST 9% and SGST 9% on your bill. Payment is by UPI after the service.
  • A block with 3 or more hours between 10 PM and 6 AM uses the night price. Quick Care is one price, day or night.
  • Female Bees are available 9 AM to 6 PM.
  • 10% off custom recurring plans with 3 or more visits a week · ₹40 travel per visit · billed weekly.
  • Hourly bookings run on your Start and End PINs, so you pay for verified time only.
