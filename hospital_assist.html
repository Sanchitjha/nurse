    A morning at the OPD, with a Bee

    Your father has a 9 am cardiology appointment and you are in Bengaluru. You book the night before. At 8:40 the Bee arrives, shows his photo ID, and your father shares the Start PIN. The Bee takes the registration queue while your father sits, hands over the medicine list at the consultation desk, collects the prescription and the test slips, and walks him to the pharmacy. You receive a message at each step. At 11:30 your father shares the End PIN and the bill arrives in your Trusbee app and on WhatsApp: 2 hours 50 minutes, itemised, GST shown.

    Questions about this service
    Can a Bee stay overnight in the ward?

    Yes, where the hospital's attendant rules allow it. Book the hours you need; night bookings are common for admissions. The Bee carries their Trusbee ID for the ward desk.

    Can the Bee collect reports without the patient present?

    Usually yes, with the registration slip and the patient's ID copy. Some hospitals require the patient; the Bee will tell you before going.

    Which hospitals do you cover?

    Every hospital in Patna, private and government, including Jai Prabha Medanta, Paras HMRI, Ruban Memorial, Big Apollo Spectra, PMCH, IGIMS and NMCH. The Bee follows that hospital's attendant rules and works alongside its staff.

    How much does a hospital attendant cost in Patna?

    ₹219 per hour + 18% GST, billed only between your Start PIN and End PIN. For a long day a fixed plan costs less: Half Day Care (6 hours), Extended Care (9 hours) or Full Shift Care (12 hours), each one price + 18% GST. Every plan price is in the free Trusbee app, and you see the total before you confirm. You pay after the visit by UPI.

    Can I book an attendant for a patient admitted for several days?

    Yes. Book day and night shifts back to back in the Trusbee app or on WhatsApp. No Bee works more than 12 hours, so a long stay is covered in shifts, and you get an update after every shift.
