What Parkinson's Care includes

  • Support while walking and moving
  • Medicines on time, as prescribed
  • Fall prevention and daily routine help
  • Support with transfers, such as getting out of bed or a chair
  • Help with exercises suggested by the physiotherapist

Who Parkinson's Care is for

People living with Parkinson's, and families who worry about falls and missed medicine times.

Questions about Parkinson's Care

Can the caregiver help with physiotherapy exercises?

They can assist with exercises your physiotherapist or doctor has advised. They do not replace a physiotherapist.

Do you help with medicine timing?

Yes. They remind and assist as per the doctor's prescription, which matters a lot with Parkinson's medicines.
