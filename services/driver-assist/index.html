    Questions about this service
    Do you provide the car?

    No. Trusbee provides the driver, not the vehicle. Your Bee drives your own car. Fuel, tolls and parking are paid by you at actuals.

    Is the driver licensed and verified?

    Every Drive Bee holds a valid driving licence and passes the same six checks as every other Bee: Aadhaar, police verification, two reference calls, an interview and training. They carry a photo ID.

    Can I book a driver for a full day or an outstation trip?

    Yes. Full Shift Care covers 12 hours, which suits a full day in the city or an outstation day trip. For anything longer, message us and we will plan the shifts so no Bee drives more than 12 hours.

    How much does a driver cost per day in Patna?

    ₹219 per hour + 18% GST, or a fixed plan: Quick Drive (3 hours) ₹399, Half Day Drive (6 hours) ₹699, Extended Drive (9 hours) ₹899 and Full Shift Drive (12 hours) ₹1,149, each + 18% GST. Night plans cost a little more. Your car's fuel, parking and tolls stay with you.

    Can I get a driver every day, or for a month?

    Yes. In the WhatsApp booking form choose daily or recurring visits: every day, Monday to Saturday, weekdays or your own days, at the same time. With 3 or more days a week the service price is 10% lower. A ₹40 travel charge applies per day, and you are billed weekly.

    Can I hire a driver for just a few hours?

    Yes. Book by the hour for a hospital visit, an airport drop or a safe drive home at night. You pay only for the time between your Start PIN and End PIN, and the bot shows the price before you confirm.
