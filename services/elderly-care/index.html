    Questions about this service
    Can I book the same Bee every time?

    Yes. Tell us in the booking chat and we assign the same Sahara Bee whenever they are available. Familiar faces matter to elders.

    What if my parent does not want a stranger at home?

    Book a short first visit with a family member present. Bees are trained to earn trust slowly, and the photo ID and uniform help. Many families start with a doctor-visit escort.

    Can the Bee take my parent to a doctor?

    Yes. The Bee accompanies by auto or cab, waits through the consultation, collects prescriptions and brings them home. Transport fares are paid by the family.

    Can I book daily or weekly visits for my parents?

    Yes. In the WhatsApp booking form choose daily or recurring visits: every day, Monday to Saturday, weekdays or your own days, at the same time. With 3 or more visits a week most plans get 10% off the service price. A ₹40 travel charge applies per visit, and you are billed weekly.

    Is a Bee a nurse?

    No. A Bee is a trained, police-verified, non-medical caregiver. A Bee keeps your parent company, helps with walks, meals and routines, reminds them of medicines and takes them to the doctor. For nursing care such as injections, dressings or catheter care, please book a qualified nurse.

    How much does elderly care cost in Patna?

    ₹199 per hour + 18% GST, or a fixed plan: Quick Care (3 hours) ₹499, Half Day Care (6 hours) ₹849, Extended Care (9 hours) ₹1,099 and Full Shift Care (12 hours) ₹1,349, each + 18% GST. You pay after the visit by UPI.
