What Patient Care includes

  • Bathing, hygiene, feeding and positioning
  • Help with movement and exercises
  • Regular updates to the family
  • Turning and positioning to help prevent bedsores
  • Help with toileting, dressing and personal hygiene

Who Patient Care is for

Families of bedridden patients, people recovering after surgery or a hospital stay, and anyone who needs steady help with daily activities.

Trained patient care at home in Your City for bedridden, post-surgery and recovering patients. Hourly, 8-hour, day and 24-hour care from ₹199.

Questions about Patient Care

Can the caregiver stay overnight?

Yes. Choose the 24-hour or day care rate, or book an 8-hour shift for the night. Tell us the schedule when you book.

Does the caregiver give medicines?

They remind and assist with medicines exactly as prescribed by your doctor. Clinical procedures are done only on a doctor's prescription.
