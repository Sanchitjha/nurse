    Questions about this service
    Can a Bee stand in a queue for me?

    Yes, for a token or a counter that does not require you personally. If your presence is needed, the Bee holds your place and calls you when it is close.

    Can the Bee buy things for me?

    Yes, with payment made by you directly by UPI to the shop or through a family member. Bees do not carry your cash or cards.

    Can a Bee accompany my mother to the bank?

    That is one of the most common bookings. The Bee helps with forms and queues while your mother handles her own money.

    How much does a personal assistant cost in Patna?

    ₹199 per hour + 18% GST, billed only between your Start PIN and End PIN, or Quick Care (3 hours) ₹499 + 18% GST for a longer errand. You pay after the work is done, by UPI.

    Can a Bee handle my registry or Nagar Nigam work?

    A Bee can go with you, stand in the queue, collect forms and tokens and follow up at the counter where the office allows it. Fees are paid by you, and a Bee never signs documents for you or acts as your legal agent.
