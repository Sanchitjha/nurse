Questions about Attendant

Can an attendant stay in the hospital?

Yes, subject to the hospital's rules for attendants. Tell us the hospital and ward when you book.

What is the difference between an attendant and a nurse?

An attendant helps with daily tasks and stays with the patient. Nursing procedures such as injections are done by trained nursing staff on a doctor's prescription.
